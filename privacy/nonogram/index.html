Nonogram privacy policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information the Android app Nonogram (“the app”) accesses, collects, uses and shares. The app is made by Handyfox (“we”, “us”), the developer shown on its Google Play page. Contact for privacy questions: kieulong1302@gmail.com.

In short

  • There's no account or sign-up. We don't ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • What you do in the app is stored on your phone.
  • Google services built into the app (ads, analytics, crash reports, remote settings) receive device and usage information, described below.
  • Ads appear only when you tap to watch a video for an extra. The main features work without watching one.

1. Permissions and data on your device

Nonogram doesn't ask for any permission that needs your approval.

The app also uses standard permissions that Android grants automatically: internet and network state, to load the videos you choose to watch and to tell you when a video needs an internet connection, and advertising ID, which the Google services below use as described.

Kept only on your device

  • Your puzzle progress: the cells you filled, whether each puzzle is solved, time played and hints used.
  • Pictures you unlocked by watching a video, your endless puzzle counts and the endless puzzle you're in the middle of.
  • Your settings (vibration, sound, timer, auto-cross, line highlight) and whether you finished the welcome screens and the tutorial.
  • Small records the app keeps for itself: the language you picked in the app, features you unlocked for a limited time and when they expire, daily free-use counters, and when the app last asked you to rate it.

We don't receive this data. It stays in the app's private storage until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include it in your device backup in your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. Google handles that backup under the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information collected by Google services in the app

The app uses the following services from Google. They collect information automatically when you use the app and send it over encrypted connections to Google, which processes it for us and, in some cases, for its own purposes. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Google AdMob (rewarded video ads)

Shows a video when you choose to watch one in exchange for an extra. AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which it can estimate your approximate location), device and app information such as model, operating system version, language and app version, and how you interact with ads. It uses this to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud. Where available, AdMob may also use Android's privacy-preserving advertising features (Topics and Attribution). Personalized ads are shown only where you've agreed or where the law allows. We ask AdMob to limit ad content to ratings suitable for teens (T) or lower.

Google User Messaging Platform (consent)

If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent form before requesting ads, so you can choose how your data is used for advertising. The form stores your choice on your device. You can change it any time in the app under Settings › Privacy options; this item appears where the law requires it.

Google Analytics for Firebase

Helps us understand how the app is used so we can improve it. It collects an app instance ID, the advertising ID where available, device information, app version, approximate location (country or region, based on IP address), how the app was installed from Google Play, and app usage such as when the app is opened, session length and screens viewed. The app also logs a few events of its own: which features you use (such as starting a puzzle, using a hint or completing a puzzle) and whether a video offer was shown, started, completed or failed, with the reason it failed (for example, no internet).

Firebase Crashlytics

Sends a report when the app crashes or hits an error, so we can fix it. A report contains technical details such as the error trace, device model, operating system version, app version and a random installation identifier.

Firebase Remote Config

Lets us change settings without an app update, for example whether a feature asks you to watch a video. It uses a Firebase installation identifier and basic device and app information such as app version and language.

Google Play In-App Review

Now and then the app may ask you to rate it in a Google Play dialog. Your rating and review go to Google Play and appear there like any other review, under your Google Play profile. The app itself doesn't receive them.

More details: Google Privacy Policy, Privacy and security in Firebase.

3. How we use and share information

We use the information above only to run the app, show the videos you choose to watch, keep the app stable, and decide what to improve. We share it only with Google as the provider of the services above, and with authorities when the law requires it. We don't share it with any other company.

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on your consent for personalized ads and for ad-related storage on your device where that's required. For analytics, crash reports, remote settings and fraud prevention, we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it.

4. Your choices

  • Change your ad consent in Settings › Privacy options (where shown).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually Settings › Privacy › Ads or Google › Ads).
  • Turn off any permission the app has in Android's app settings at any time.
  • Uninstall the app to stop all collection and remove the data stored on your device.

5. Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. We keep user-level analytics data for no more than 14 months. Crash reports are kept for 90 days. Google may keep advertising data according to its own policies.

We don't know who you are, because the app has no accounts. If you'd like us to delete analytics or crash data linked to your device, or you have any other privacy request, email kieulong1302@gmail.com and we'll help.

6. Security

Google's services send data over encrypted connections. Data the app stores on your device is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected, contact us and we'll help delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top. If a change affects how your data is handled in a significant way, we'll also mention it in the app's release notes on Google Play.
